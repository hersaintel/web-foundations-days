About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple, fast way to capture ideas, reminders and to-do lists. Open it, type your note, and get back to what you were doing.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and click into the "New note" box.
  2. Type your note.
  3. Press "Add Note" to save it.
  4. Find your saved notes in the "Your notes" list.
  5. Click "Delete" on any note you no longer need.

Features

  • Add notes instantly
  • See a running count of your notes
  • Simple, distraction-free layout
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common actions
  • Works on phones and laptops
  • Notes are saved in your browser

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you are typing
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move to the previous field or button
